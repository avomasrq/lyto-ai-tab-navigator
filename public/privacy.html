This Privacy Policy describes how the Argos browser extension and the Argos web service ("Argos", "we", "us", "our") collect, use, store, share and protect your information. Argos is an AI powered research and browser assistant. It is published by Argos and can be reached at info@tryargos.cc. This policy covers the Chrome extension identified above, the website at tryargos.cc, and the Argos command line tool.

The single purpose of the extension is to act on your browser on your behalf: to read the page you are looking at, to perform the browser actions you ask for, and to research topics for you. Every kind of data described below is handled for that purpose alone.

1. Data we collect

We collect and process the minimum needed to provide the service. The categories below use the same names as the Chrome Web Store data disclosures.

CategoryWhat it is, and why we need it
Personally identifiable information Your email address, collected when you create an account, used to authenticate you and to contact you about your account.
Authentication information Cookies for sites you have open, and encrypted OAuth access tokens for integrations you connect yourself (for example Google Sheets, Google Docs, Gmail, Airtable). A task you start can keep running after you close your browser, so the same signed in session has to be available to Argos on the server side. Cookies are read while you are signed in and the Argos panel is open, and they are passed only to your own Argos account. You can also hand a specific site over yourself in Settings, Browser Sessions, and remove any stored session there at any time.
Personal communications The messages you send to Argos, the AI responses you receive, and any files you attach to a conversation. Stored while your account is active so that your conversation history is there when you come back.
Website content Page text, page structure (DOM context, links, form field labels) and screenshots of the tab, captured when you ask Argos to act on or answer about a page. Used to carry out that request.
Web history The URL and title of the tab you point Argos at, and of pages it opens while carrying out your task. Used to give the model context and to show you what it did.
User activity The browser actions Argos performs for you (navigation, clicks, scrolling, typing) and usage metadata: timestamps, session counts and request counts. Used to run your task, to enforce plan limits and to monitor system health.
Local extension data Your preferences, configuration and active session state, stored on your device through Chrome's storage API. Not sent to our servers unless a specific feature needs it.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not collect health information, location, names, or phone numbers.
  • We do not collect financial and payment information. Payments are processed by our provider, Polar. We never see or store your card details.
  • We never read, store or fill password fields, and we do not use the Credential Management API. Chrome's own autofill populates credential fields, and your explicit approval is required before any form is submitted.
  • We do not scrape pages in the background. Page content, page structure and screenshots are captured only when you ask Argos to act on a page.

2. How we use and handle your data

Your data is used to deliver the feature you asked for, and for nothing else. Concretely, the browser capabilities Argos requests are used as follows:

  • Automation and control. The Chrome DevTools Protocol, through chrome.debugger and chrome.scripting, performs browser actions on your behalf: navigation, clicks, scrolling and text input.
  • Reading the page. activeTab and host access let Argos read the page you point it at so it can answer about it or act on it.
  • Tabs. The tabs, tabGroups and webNavigation APIs list your open tabs in the panel for context, switch between them, and organise tab groups from natural language commands.
  • Cookies. The cookies API carries your own signed in session into your own server side tasks, as described above.
  • Clipboard and downloads. Clipboard write access pastes data into web applications and copies results out. The Downloads API saves generated files and screenshots to your Downloads folder.
  • Notifications. System alerts tell you when a background or research task has finished.

We do not use your data for advertising, for profiling, or to build a profile of you across sites. We do not use your conversations to train our own models.

3. How we share your data

We do not sell, rent, trade or lease your personal data to anyone, under any circumstances. To provide the core AI features we transmit your prompts and the page context needed for your request to the following processors:

  • Google Gemini, for AI responses and browser task reasoning.
  • Anthropic Claude, for AI responses and deep research tasks.
  • Perplexity, for web search and real time information retrieval.
  • Supabase, our database and authentication host, described in section 5.
  • Polar, our payment provider, which handles subscription billing. It receives your billing details directly and we do not see your card data.

Each processor handles the data under its own privacy policy. Passwords and payment card data are never sent to the AI providers.

Encrypted integration tokens are transmitted only to the platform you connected them to, in order to execute your commands, and to no one else.

We may disclose data if we are legally required to do so, or to protect the rights, safety and property of Argos or its users.

4. Limited use of the data we receive

Our use of data received from Google APIs and from Chrome extension APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • We use the data only to provide and improve the user facing features described in this policy.
  • We do not transfer or use the data for purposes unrelated to the single purpose of the extension, except as required by law, to comply with legal process, or with your explicit consent.
  • We do not sell the data, and we do not transfer it to data brokers, advertisers or information resellers.
  • We do not use or transfer the data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
  • Humans do not read your data, except with your explicit permission to debug a problem you reported, where required by law, or on aggregated and anonymised data for security and system health.

5. Where your data is stored, and how it is secured

Account data, conversation history, generated files and usage metadata are stored on servers hosted by Supabase, on AWS infrastructure in the ap-northeast-2 region. Generated files are stored on a persistent volume attached to our application host, Railway. Local preferences and session state stay on your own device in Chrome's local storage.

All data is encrypted in transit with HTTPS and TLS, and encrypted at rest by our storage providers. Integration OAuth tokens are encrypted before they are stored. Access to production systems is restricted to the operators who need it. Page content and screenshots sent for a single request are processed to answer that request and are not retained as a separate archive.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach ever affects your personal data, we will notify affected users and the relevant authorities as required by law.

6. How long we keep it

Conversation history, generated files and usage metadata are retained while your account is active. You can delete individual conversations and files at any time from inside the product. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by writing to info@tryargos.cc. All personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days of account deletion. Stored browser sessions can be removed at any time in Settings, Browser Sessions, and are deleted immediately when you do.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Access. Ask for the personal data we hold about you, and export your conversation history.
  • Correction. Have inaccurate data corrected.
  • Deletion. Have your account and all associated data deleted.
  • Objection and restriction. Object to or ask us to restrict a particular kind of processing.
  • Withdrawal of consent. Disconnect any integration at any time in Settings, which revokes the stored token.

To exercise any of these rights, write to info@tryargos.cc. We will respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights under the GDPR or the CCPA, and this policy is how we meet them.

You can stop all data collection at any time by signing out, disabling the extension, or removing it from Chrome. You can also simply not use the features that send page context.

8. Children's privacy

Argos is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, write to info@tryargos.cc and we will delete it promptly.

9. International transfers

Argos operates internationally, and the processors listed in section 3 may process your data outside your own country, including in the United States and in South Korea. Where such a transfer happens, it is covered by the safeguards our processors provide, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, the "Last updated" date above is revised, and a material change is announced in the product or by email. Continued use of Argos after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact us

For any privacy question, request or concern, write to info@tryargos.cc. We aim to reply within 2 business days.
